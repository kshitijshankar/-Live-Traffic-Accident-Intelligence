How Risk Score Works

The risk model derives a score (0 to 100) per township based on the following algorithm:

  • Volume Weight (40%): Township incident density normalized against the highest volume location in the county.
  • Severity Index (40%): Average severity of events in the township (Critical=4, Severe=3, Moderate=2, Minor=1).
  • Traffic Congestion Weight (20%): Percentage share of traffic/disabled vehicle alerts out of total emergency events.
LOW (0 - 25) MODERATE (26 - 50) HIGH (51 - 75) CRITICAL (76 - 100)
